I recorded this on a physical iPhone, current iOS, from the Home Screen icon. Kentledge is a personal ledger for this month’s named charges. You type a name, an amount, and when it hits again. The home list shows the monthly total and what you free if you cancel a charge — but only after you put that money on another charge you keep, or into a savings name. Audience: people who want this month’s load in one place, not a logo list. No bank login, no App Store subscriptions, no IAP.

No account, so there is no login / signup / delete to show, and no demo password. Charge names stay on this device. Nothing is published, so report and block are not in the app.

Walkthrough:

Onboarding: See this month’s bill / Cancel has a cost of honesty / Put the money somewhere / Saved by cutting. I used Show my charges. Skip lands on the same home list.

Empty home says Add a charge. Form: Name, Amount, Period, Next charge, then Add. Header shows This month and Saved by cutting with matching figure size. Charge rows show period and next date — tap to cancel, hold to edit. Cancel [biggest] does the same. After a cancel, put the freed money on another charge or New savings name; Undo cancel puts the charge back. Cuts lists seated cuts and yearly savings. What if… ranks remaining charges, next calendar dates, year estimate, Share. Home also has Share. Settings: currency, local reminder, savings names, Contact Kentledge, Re-run onboarding, Reset all data.

Reviewer setup: install, open, Show my charges or Skip, use sample charges (Streaming, Gym pass, Cloud backup, Emergency fund), cancel one, place the money, open Cuts and What if…, then Settings.

Permissions: we do not ask for camera, mic, contacts, location, or tracking. The plist camera line is “This app does not use the camera.” Local reminder is only an hour integer in Settings.

Charges and preferences are on-device storage. Support: https://kentledge-beam.pro/contact-us. Privacy: https://kentledge-beam.pro/privacy-policy. No login vendor, ads, analytics, or model API.

Same screens in every region. Locale only changes currency glyphs and date formats.

Not a licensed finance product and we do not ship a third-party catalog.
