Kentledge is a personal ledger for this month’s named charges. Add each bill with a name, amount, and next date; the home list shows the monthly total — not a smeared year.

• Home shows This month and Saved by cutting, with Cuts / What if… / Settings
• Add a charge with Name, Amount, Period, and Next charge
• Each row shows period and next date; tap to cancel, hold to edit
• Freed money must land on another charge you keep, or a savings name — then the total drops
• Cuts history lists every seated cut and yearly savings
• What if I cut… ranks remaining charges, shows next calendar dates, year estimate, and Share
• Share this month from home or Insights
• Settings: currency, local reminder, savings names, Contact Kentledge, Re-run onboarding, Reset all data
• Charges stay on this device

There is no account and no bank login. Saved-by-cutting is the money you placed after a cancel.
