Version 1.0 is the first public release.

On first launch, four pages cover See this month’s bill, Cancel has a cost of honesty, Put the money somewhere, and Saved by cutting. Skip opens the same home list.

Home shows This month and Saved by cutting. Add a charge with Name, Amount, Period and Next charge. Tap a charge to cancel it, then put the freed money on another charge or a savings name. What if I cut… ranks remaining charges. Settings holds currency, reminder, savings names, Contact Kentledge, Re-run onboarding and Reset all data.
